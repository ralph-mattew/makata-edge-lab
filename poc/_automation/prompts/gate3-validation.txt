ROI formula: EV = P(success) × Impact - P(failure) × (Time_wasted / 10)
- Impact: 1-10 scale (10 = game-changing, 1 = minor improvement)
- Time_wasted: Hours spent if experiment fails
- Divide by 10 to normalize time to 0-10 scale

Example: If idea has 60% confidence, 8/10 impact, and 40 hours at risk:
EV = 0.6 × 8 - 0.4 × (40/10) = 4.8 - 1.6 = +3.2 (APPROVE)

Be realistic: Most ideas fail. Require EV >0 to approve.
